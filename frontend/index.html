🧠 Quantitative Pipeline Architecture

Forecasting exact absolute price movements usually fails due to financial market noise and non-stationarity. Our architecture models directional upside probability subject to dynamic risk controls, combining machine learning classification with rigorous rule-based safety screening.

1. Reliable Market Ingestion

Connects to Indian Equities across NSE and BSE using calibrated .NS tickers. Daily OHLCV price bars and turnover are ingested and cached locally via persistent serialized containers to ensure sub-second response times and zero rate limits.

Tickers: RELIANCE.NS, TCS.NS, HDFCBANK.NS, INFY.NS...
Benchmark: ^NSEI (Nifty 50) & ^NSEBANK
Turnover: (Close * Volume) / 10,000,000 (Rs. Crores)

2. Multi-Factor Feature Matrix

Transforms raw price action into 25+ orthogonal features across Momentum, Trend, Volatility, Volume Accumulation, and Market Regime:

  • Trend: EMA 20, 50, 200 ratios & 5-day slope
  • Momentum: Wilder's RSI(14), Stochastic, MACD Histogram
  • Volatility: Average True Range (ATR), Bollinger Bands (%B, Bandwidth)
  • Volume: 20-Day Volume Ratio, On-Balance Volume (OBV) slope
  • Regime: Nifty 50 20-day return & Bullish Regime flag

3. Triple-Barrier Target Labeling

Rather than raw regression, we formulate a classification problem with a 10-day holding horizon:

Target = 1 if:
Max_High(+10 days) >= Entry + 4.0% AND
Min_Low(+10 days) > Entry - 2.5%
Target = 0 otherwise

This prevents labeling setups as "Buy" if a premature stop-loss would have been triggered before reaching the target.

4. LightGBM Time-Series Split

Trained using strict Walk-Forward Chronological Validation (80% train on past data, 20% test on recent out-of-sample data). Random shuffling is strictly disallowed to prevent future lookahead leakage.

5. Rule Engine & Safety Screen

Raw ML probability must pass four deterministic safety rules before receiving a Buy recommendation:

  • Trend Filter: Price > 50-day EMA and Price > 200-day EMA
  • Liquidity: Average 20-day turnover >= Rs. 10–20 Crore
  • Model Conviction: LightGBM probability >= 60%
  • Risk:Reward: Target / Stop-Loss ratio >= 1.50

6. Actionable Trade Execution

Calculates dynamic entry, ATR stop-loss (1.5x ATR below entry), dual profit targets, and mathematically sound position sizing for any portfolio size (defaulting to Rs. 1,00,000 risking max 1.5%).
